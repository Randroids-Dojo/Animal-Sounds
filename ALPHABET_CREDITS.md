# Alphabet artwork

OpenMoji artwork is from [OpenMoji 16.0.0](https://github.com/hfg-gmuend/openmoji/tree/16.0.0), licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). These SVGs are used unchanged.

Original project illustrations: alligator, blue, circle, diamond, fork, green, igloo, iguana, jam, narwhal, oval, pink, quail, queen, quilt, quince, red, square, triangle, ukulele, urchin, violet, vulture, water, x-ray-tetra, xenops, xerus, xylophone, yak, yellow, yogurt, zero, zigzag, zipper, zucchini.

The X animal names were checked against the [National Aquarium](https://aqua.org/stories/2018-06-18-x-is-for-x-ray-tetra), [Animal Diversity Web](https://animaldiversity.org/accounts/Xerus_rutilus/), and [British Museum bird guide](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Guide_to_the_Gallery_of_Birds_in_the_Department_of_Zoology_of_the_British_Museum_(Natural_History)_(IA_guidetogallery00britrich).pdf).
